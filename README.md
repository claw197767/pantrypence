# PantryPence

### Monthly Profit & Margin Audits

PantryPence helps independent UK food and drink businesses understand where their profit margins are going.

## What we review

- Supplier invoice costs
- Menu and ingredient margins
- Waste and ordering patterns
- Till sales and product performance

## Your monthly report

Every month you receive:

- A human-reviewed financial analysis
- Three practical actions for your business
- Estimated financial impact in GBP (£)

## Our approach

Built for busy independent hospitality businesses.

No complicated dashboards. Just clear insights and practical recommendations.
